# Solarae financial-model rebuild — 11 September 2026

## Purpose

This note replaces the earlier **A$380,000 DFS/PoC**, **A$2.0m plant** and **A$16.20 contribution/panel** headline figures as capital-decision inputs. They were useful placeholders for discussing the concept, but they are not sufficiently evidenced for an investment case.

The replacement is a transparent pre-feasibility model: it shows the cash hurdle that a 45,000-panel/year line would need to clear, separates a validation programme from a plant decision, and identifies the quotes and contracts needed to turn it into a bankable model.

## What changed

| Earlier treatment | Rebuild treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A$4.20/panel direct labour plus A$260k “core labour” in fixed opex | One fully loaded staffing line in fixed cash opex until a time study proves genuinely variable labour | Avoids double counting and makes the staffing plan testable. |
| A$16.20/panel contribution before site costs | Cash break-even revenue of **A$20.38/panel** before any return on plant capital | A contribution margin is not a plant return. |
| A$2.0m plant base | Legacy screening placeholder only; no valuation, IRR or payback is presented | There are no configuration-specific vendor quotes, installation scope or working-capital calculation. |
| A$380k “DFS/PoC” | **A$0.20m–A$0.22m first gate**, with only later, optional work funded after results | A mineral-style DFS is premature before feed, product, process, site and vendor evidence exists. |
| One three-year financial outcome | Cash-hurdle cases, with base/low/high revenue inputs explicitly marked uncontracted | It prevents an unvalidated upside case from being read as a forecast. |

## Bottom-up screening case

All figures below are in AUD, ex GST, and are **planning assumptions—not quotes, contracts, forecasts or guidance**.

### Capacity and operating boundary

| Driver | Working input | Basis / status |
|---|---:|---|
| Accepted modules | 45,000/year | Retained as an illustrative first-line design throughput; no feed contract. |
| Average module mass | 28kg | Engineering planning midpoint within 20–32kg range; must be measured by cohort. |
| Scheduled operating time | 1,875h/year | 250 days × 7.5 hours; management assumption. |
| Average throughput | 24 panels/hour | 45,000 ÷ 1,875; needs timed campaign evidence. |
| Non-labour variable cost | A$9.60/panel | Collection A$6.40 + utilities/consumables A$1.80 + product/residue/wear A$1.40. Each needs supplier or carrier quotes. |
| Fixed cash opex | A$485,000/year | Legacy screening allowance for staffing, site, maintenance and compliance. Reclassified as one fixed operating cost pending an FTE plan. |

The A$9.60/panel non-labour cost retains the earlier component estimates solely as a *screening assumption*. It does **not** claim those costs have been measured.

### Cash hurdle

\[
\text{cash revenue hurdle/panel} = \text{non-labour variable cost/panel} + \frac{\text{annual fixed cash opex}}{\text{accepted panels}}
\]

\[
\text{A$20.38} = \text{A$9.60} + \frac{\text{A$485,000}}{45,000}
\]

| Measure | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Cash break-even revenue | 9.60 + 485,000 ÷ 45,000 | **A$20.38/panel** |
| Cash EBITDA at A$20 total realised revenue | (20.00 − 20.38) × 45,000 | **−A$17,000/year** |
| Cash EBITDA at A$25 total realised revenue | (25.00 − 20.38) × 45,000 | **A$208,000/year** |
| Cash EBITDA at A$30 total realised revenue | (30.00 − 20.38) × 45,000 | **A$433,000/year** |

“Total realised revenue” means the **signed service price plus money actually settled for products**, after payability, deductions, freight and off-spec charges. It is not the theoretical metal content of a panel.

### Capital-return hurdle (illustrative only)

If, and only if, a later vendor-quoted installed plant required A$2.0m of deployable capital, a simple 15% pre-tax cash return target would add A$300,000/year, or A$6.67/panel, to the cash hurdle.

| Measure | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---:|
| Cash break-even revenue | as above | A$20.38/panel |
| Return allowance at 15% on A$2.0m | 300,000 ÷ 45,000 | A$6.67/panel |
| Indicative revenue hurdle | 20.38 + 6.67 | **A$27.04/panel** |

This is a screening test, not a target return, valuation or payback calculation. It excludes depreciation, tax, debt service, ramp, working capital, sustaining capital and failure risk. Those items will raise the true funding requirement.

## Validation programme: small first gate, not DFS

The programme budget below is a work-breakdown estimate. Every line is a management allowance until a scope, partner and vendor quote are obtained. It deliberately does **not** buy a pilot plant, a bespoke robotics cell, a full flotation loop or a site fit-out before the simple commercial questions are answered.

| First-gate work package | Allowance | What it must deliver |
|---|---:|---|
| Representative feed and carrier quotes | A$30,000 | Dated samples, condition log, handling cost and initial carrier quotes. |
| Bench preparation and controlled-release rig | A$65,000 | Safe fixtures, basic instrumentation and repeatable test protocol. |
| Independent assay and buyer samples | A$40,000 | Assays, sample preparation and indicative buyer terms. |
| HSE, waste classification and site-path memo | A$25,000 | Safety boundary, residue route and permit-path review. |
| Engineering, data and independent review | A$35,000 | Test design, cost ledger, decision memo and limited FTO scan. |
| **First-gate base** | **A$195,000** | Before contingency. |
| Contingency (10%) | **A$19,500** | Released only against defined variance. |
| **First-gate envelope** | **A$214,500** | **Not a quote; refresh after RFQs.** |

Only if the first gate passes, the sponsor can elect a second, separately budgeted work package for full-module campaigns, a wet-recovery/flotation trial, extended buyer lots, detailed engineering and a vendor RFQ package. It should not be authorised in advance. A sensible planning range is **A$150k–A$220k for the first gate**, depending on in-kind lab, panel and partner support.

The correct immediate ask is therefore **a staged A$0.20m base / A$0.215m including contingency first gate**, released against proof gates. It is not a “DFS”, and it does not authorise plant spend.

## External comparators used cautiously

- The Waste Management and Resource Recovery Association of Australia’s 2026 submission says Queensland pilot logistics accounted for more than 60% of total expenses and cites recycling costs of A$10–20/panel. This is a sector comparator; it is **not** a Solarae revenue quote or an operating-cost quote for a particular site.
- The Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water says the A$24.7m national pilot will pay collection/recycling providers according to location and specifically seeks data on transport cost. It does **not** publish a Solarae service fee.
- The Fair Work Ombudsman records a National Minimum Wage of A$26.44/hour from 1 July 2026. It is a statutory floor, not a fully loaded operating labour cost or an applicable award rate.

## Decision thresholds before a plant investment memorandum

No plant capital recommendation should be made until all of the following are evidence-backed:

1. Feed: contracted tonnes/panels, rejection rules, collection geography, price and termination terms sufficient for the chosen throughput.
2. Products: independent assays and at least three paid buyer settlements showing grade, recovery, payable price, deductions, freight and off-spec handling.
3. Operations: timed line balance by panel cohort; named FTE roster; labour burden; availability, rework, utilities, water and residue mass balance.
4. Capital: vendor quotations, installation/site scope, commissioning, contingency, working capital and sustaining capital.
5. Approvals: site, HSE, waste/residue, insurance, FTO and buyer/customer contractual pathway.

Until then, the only economically honest conclusion is: **fund validation only; do not fund a commercial plant on the existing numbers.**
