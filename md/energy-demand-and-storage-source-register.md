# Energy-system context: source register

**Purpose.** This register captures the energy-demand and storage references named in the Antigravity brief. They are context for plant design, electricity price risk and the choice of low-temperature processing. They are not evidence of solar-panel recycling demand and are not booked as Solarae revenue.

## Sources located in the brief

| Source | Authority | What it can support | Use in Solarae | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oxford Economics Australia, *Data centre energy consumption report* | Prepared for AEMO’s 2025 IASR scenarios | AEMO scenario inputs on data-centre electricity demand and system planning | Electricity-system context; possible sensitivity to future load growth | **Reference identified; values not yet extracted** |

## What the sources do — and do not — prove

The sources support a narrow conclusion: Australian electricity demand, renewable generation, storage and network capacity should be considered when a new processing plant is designed. They do not establish Solarae’s electricity price, annual load, battery requirement, emissions intensity or commercial demand. Those values require a site, equipment schedule, tariff and measured pilot data.

| Claim type | Evidence available now | Solarae treatment |
|---|---|---|
| System demand is changing | AEMO/Oxford scenario report | Context only; no recycling-demand proxy |
| New loads compete for capacity | CEFC transition report | Design consideration; test connection and tariff assumptions |
| Storage may shift load or manage peaks | CEFC report | Option to test after the plant load profile is measured |
| Battery cost and embodied footprint | **No extracted source in project** | Open research item; do not state a trend as fact |
| Solarae energy cost per panel | **No site or meter data** | Pilot measurement gate |

## Evidence gap to close

Before the financial model is presented as investment-grade, extract from both reports:

1. the relevant scenario years and demand measures;
2. the stated data-centre load assumptions and limitations;
3. storage capacity, duration, cost and system-value measures where reported;
4. the distinction between national-system demand and a project-level industrial load; and
5. page-level citations for every number used.

Then replace the current illustrative electricity and storage assumptions with a site-specific schedule: connected load, operating hours, peak demand, tariff components, renewable supply, storage dispatch, water and thermal loads, and measured kWh per accepted panel.
