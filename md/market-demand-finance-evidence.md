## Pitch and finance framework boundary

The report uses the useful parts of the pitch references—one-page ask, explicit
stage, use of funds, proof threshold and downside/base/upside cases—but does not
present them as external evidence. Financial numbers remain labelled as
published comparator, quoted input, model assumption or pilot measurement.

The base case excludes grants, future levies, carbon revenue and uncontracted
stewardship payments. The A$380,000 request is a DFS/PoC validation budget; a
pilot plant is a later decision contingent on measured throughput, product
acceptance, safety, freight and buyer settlement.
